# shopping-behavior

## Analytical Questions

What are 6 analytical questions you can ask about this dataset?
**"My Analytical Questions"**
1 Which season sees the highest total purchase amount, and which season sees the lowest?
2 Which age group of customers makes the most purchases?
3 Which items or product categories are the top sellers?
4 Do discounts significantly impact sales volume?
5 Which geographic regions have the highest volume of customer purchases?
6 What recurring patterns exist in the data, and can we predict the next high-sales period?
